# Dock-source licensing and attribution

Copyright (c) 2026 DionLabs. Original contributions are covered by
[DOCK-LICENSE-MIT](DOCK-LICENSE-MIT). The
[per-file ledger](packaging/dock-source-attribution.json) records the exact scope.
Its original compilation is also covered by the same grant; its own hash is bound
by the separate export allowlist to avoid a recursive hash.

`local-expression` grants our original expression only. `mixed-upstream-adaptation`
grants our additions while preserving upstream snippets, selectors and inherited
configuration under their existing MIT terms. `verified-upstream-MIT` is inherited
material and receives no DionLabs copyright or new original-contribution grant.
Public identifiers, APIs, formats and dependency facts are not claimed as ours.
This is a content and source-reference review, not proof of exclusive authorship.

The appearance source and notice match Omarchy commit
c668141e9c42b13c80c9ca4ea108e11708c5e8a5. Shell adaptation references are pinned to
b83d3df0840504299d3099ebf391eb8110527edd. Both retain the upstream notice naming
its copyright holder: [shell notice](open-source/licenses/omarchy-shell-LICENSE.txt)
and [appearance notice](build/arch-arm/dock/omarchy-profile/upstream/LICENSE).
The adaptation scripts contain upstream matching text; the battery widget and
Hyprland profile are conservatively classified as mixed adaptations. Nothing in
this grant replaces those notices or claims the upstream project as DionLabs work.

The publication includes source and review metadata only. Upstream fonts/artwork,
generated shell payloads, private graphics binaries, firmware and user configuration
are excluded. Recipes that acquire or copy those inputs do not clear redistribution
of their outputs. Preserve this statement and the notices when distributing the
source. The published boot preview has a separate source scope and remains unchanged.
